OPEN VOCABULARY EXTENSION V1

The additional 2,974 headwords were selected from the following openly
licensed resources. For 2,956 entries, an English part of speech and a
Japanese WordNet concept matched and the same Japanese expression was present
in EJDict-hand. The remaining 18 A1 foundation entries were manually checked
against CEFR-J for headword and part of speech and against EJDict-hand for the
Japanese definition. The resulting selection, short quiz prompts, ranks, and
Eiken-equivalent bands are project adaptations.
